Emulation

Mesoscopic emulation, not molecular dynamics. Seeded subset, cap 300. The Python Monte Carlo is the reference.

What this is not. Not molecular dynamics of the 2575 kg magnet. Not an MRI SNR measurement (the Swiss-roll gain is a heuristic). Not a certified magnet design. Not a Florida-Bitter plate drawing. Round graded holes and a uniform rho(T) are continuum assumptions. Contact resistance is sampled only in the emulation.
